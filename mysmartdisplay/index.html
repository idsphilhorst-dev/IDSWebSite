my Smart Display

my Smart Display is a home information display from Integral Design Software. Design a layout on an Android tablet, then show it on a Google TV or a Windows PC on the same network.

Place the widgets you want on the screen:

  • Clock
  • Calendar
  • Photo slideshow
  • Outdoor weather
  • Airqio indoor air quality

Calendars can come from Google, Microsoft or Outlook, Yahoo, or an ICS link. Google and Microsoft access is read-only, so the app can show events and cannot change them.

Pairing and layout sync stay on your home network, between your own tablet and the display. Sign-in tokens stay on each device. Integral Design Software does not run an account server for the app.
